About Me

Profile

A motivated MSc Economics (Data Analytics) student with a strong quantitative foundation in statistics, econometrics and data analysis.

Experienced in data compilation, statistical analysis, business research and translating quantitative findings into actionable insights through internships at Tata Power Renewable Energy Ltd. and NITI Aayog. My experience spans renewable energy, public-sector innovation, economic research and academic data analysis. My interests lie at the intersection of data analytics, economics, finance and business decision-making.
