About

I'm an MSc Economics (Data Analytics) student at Symbiosis School of Economics, Pune, working at the intersection of econometrics, financial modelling and business research. My work turns quantitative data — from stock indices to primary field surveys — into insights that can actually inform decisions.

My recent work spans asymmetric-beta modelling of Indian sectoral indices using ARIMAX, a cross-country econometric study of unemployment determinants in STATA, and a 100-respondent primary survey on pedestrianisation and vendor income in Mumbai. I'm drawn to problems where economic theory, data and applied statistics meet.
